\section{Basis of Estimate Narrative}

\subsection{Labor Hour and Rate Basis}

% WRITER NOTE: Tailor the hourly rate justification to the specific solicitation labor rate caps or regional wage determinations once published.
The labor estimate is derived from the 6-month Phase I technical work plan in Volume 2. The Principal Investigator (480 hours) leads architecture, customer coordination, and the final report. The Senior Embedded Engineer (360 hours) implements the \texttt{\#![no\_std]} Rust kernel, C-compatible FFI, host regression harness, and ARM Cortex-M7 target build. Hourly rates reflect fiscal year 2026 loaded salaries for the National Capital Region and prevailing rates for defense SBIR safety-critical software work.

\subsection{Equipment and Material Basis}

Equipment costs are based on publicly available manufacturer and distributor pricing for Pixhawk 6X-class development kits, professional JTAG/SWD debug probes, and high-speed USB logic analyzers. Quantities maintain at least one active host/target equivalence testbed and one independent fault-injection testbed throughout the period, plus a spare configuration backup.

\subsection{Other Direct Costs (ODCs) Basis}

The \$13,880.00 Other Direct Costs line funds Cloud Emulation and HIL Digital Twin Test Infrastructure required to execute the eight design-of-experiments (DOEs) in Volume 2 at realistic scale. This includes on-demand cloud compute for Monte Carlo SIL parameter sweeps, containerized firmware-in-the-loop regression runners, and persistent artifact storage for host/target equivalence traces. The estimate is sized for six months of moderate cloud consumption (compute, egress, and object storage) and will be tracked as a discrete direct cost line item. Without this infrastructure, deterministic 1,000-iteration HIL equivalence and swarm-scale DOE-05/DOE-07 stress tests would be impractical within the Phase I period.

\subsection{Indirect Rate and Fixed Fee Basis}

Fringe is applied to direct labor; overhead is applied to direct labor plus fringe; G\&A is applied to the subtotal of direct labor, fringe, overhead, materials, and ODCs. The rates are provisional for fiscal year 2026 and will be documented in the certified cost or pricing data. The fixed fee is 10.027\% of total estimated cost, equal to \$18,226.40, within the SBIR Phase I fee ceiling.
