How ReluLayer™ Protects Autonomous Flight

ReluLayer™ sits between the client AI flight controller and the actuator layer, mathematically enforcing that every output vector remains inside the certifiable flight envelope.

Runaway AI Interdiction

When a client neural network or guidance loop issues an unsafe command, ReluLayer™ clamps the vector to the safe flight envelope in 0.199 µs—before actuators move.

Simplex / Runtime Assurance Architecture
