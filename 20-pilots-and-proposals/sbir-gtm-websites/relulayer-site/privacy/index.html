Privacy and Data Policy

Effective Date: August 3, 2026

Atelier Logic Group LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) dba ReluLayer™ respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you share with us.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Business Contact Information: name, email address, phone number, organization, and job title when you submit an inquiry, SBLO lead form, or request a briefing.
  • Technical Information: IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited via standard server logs and analytics.
  • Communications: records of correspondence with our team, including proposals, contracts, and support requests.

We do not collect sensitive personal information (e.g., health, biometrics, government ID numbers, precise geolocation) through our websites. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16.

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide product or contracting information.
  • Evaluate potential partnerships, SBIR/STTR opportunities, and OEM engagements.
  • Maintain security, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.
  • Improve our website and communication workflows.

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising or other monetary consideration.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Trusted service providers who assist in hosting, analytics, and communications, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Government or regulatory authorities when required by law or in connection with national security contracting requirements.

Third-Party Services

Our websites use Google Fonts to serve typography. Google may receive your IP address and browser information when fonts are loaded. We do not use advertising cookies, social-media trackers, or analytics pixels that identify individuals.

Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect the information we collect. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain business contact information for as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry, fulfill the purposes described above, and comply with legal, tax, and contracting obligations. Server logs are retained for up to 12 months unless longer retention is required for security or legal purposes.

Your Choices and Rights

U.S. State Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA, Colorado CPA, Virginia CDPA, Connecticut CTDPA, Utah UCPA, Texas TDPSA)

Depending on your state of residence, you may have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect and how we use it.
  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Delete your personal information, subject to legal and contractual exceptions.
  • Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell or share personal information).
  • Obtain a copy of your personal information in a portable format, where applicable.
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising your privacy rights.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the information below. We will verify your request before responding.

European and UK Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process your personal information on the basis of legitimate interest (to respond to your inquiry and evaluate business opportunities) or, where required, consent.

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information.
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Erase your personal information in certain circumstances.
  • Restrict processing of your personal information.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Data portability — receive your information in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

International Data Transfers

If you contact us from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States. We rely on appropriate safeguards, including standard contractual clauses where required, to protect your information.
