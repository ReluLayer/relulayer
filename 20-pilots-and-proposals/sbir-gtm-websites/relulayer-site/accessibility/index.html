Accessibility Statement

Effective Date: August 26, 2026

Atelier Logic Group LLC dba ReluLayer™ is committed to ensuring that our websites are accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We aim to meet or exceed the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA success criteria, and we design our content with U.S. federal and international procurement standards in mind.

Conformance Targets

  • WCAG 2.1 Level AA — the baseline for ADA Title III and state anti-discrimination compliance.
  • U.S. Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act — for federal and defense procurement audiences.
  • EN 301 549 — the European standard referenced by the EU Web Accessibility Directive and European Accessibility Act.

What We Do

  • Use semantic HTML and ARIA landmarks so pages work with screen readers and keyboard navigation.
  • Provide skip links to bypass repetitive navigation.
  • Associate all form labels programmatically with their inputs.
  • Honor prefers-reduced-motion for users who choose to minimize animation.
  • Maintain color-contrast ratios that meet WCAG 2.1 AA thresholds for text and interactive elements.
  • Test keyboard operability across navigation, forms, and interactive demos.

Known Limitations

Some interactive technical demonstrations use SVG graphics and animation to illustrate deterministic safety concepts. These demos include text alternatives, aria-labels, and reduced-motion fallbacks, but may still present challenges for some assistive technologies. We continue to improve these experiences and welcome feedback.

Third-Party Content

Our websites may link to third-party documents, partner sites, or government systems that are outside our control. We encourage those providers to maintain accessible experiences and are happy to route feedback to them when appropriate.

Feedback and Assistance

If you encounter an accessibility barrier or need assistance accessing any content, please contact us. We will make reasonable efforts to provide the information in an accessible format.
