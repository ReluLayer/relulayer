### Commercial Embed License (Proprietary Hardware / ASICs / FPGAs)

The open-core licenses (MIT / Apache-2.0) allow broad commercial utilization. However, a **ReluLayer™ Commercial Embed License** is mandatory for organizations seeking:

1. Warranty and indemnification coverage for flight-critical, medical, or life-safety hardware.
2. Custom Board Support Packages (BSPs) for specialized real-time silicon (ARM Cortex-R, RISC-V RV64GC, TI Hercules, Infineon AURIX).
3. Relief from open-source attribution requirements in closed consumer or defense equipment.
